{{% columns %}}
- ### Your progress, your server {anchor=false}
  ReSteep runs a small server on your PC that stands in for Ubisoft's online services. Levels, the store,
  challenges, rewards, leaderboards, replays and cloud saves keep working.

- ### Ride with friends {anchor=false}
  Host for your friends or join theirs, on Windows or Linux. Each player gets their own account and
  progress on the server.

- ### An in-game menu {anchor=false}
  Press **N** in the game: gameplay and world settings, the time of day, a free-flying camera, your mods,
  your profile and the server.
{{% /columns %}}

{{% columns %}}
- ### Mods {anchor=false}
  New gear, textures and texts, new maps and challenges, music, animations and gameplay changes. Install
  them from the launcher with one click.

- ### A world editor {anchor=false}
  Place, move and remove objects in Steep's world from inside the game. Your server keeps the edits.

- ### Made to be modded {anchor=false}
  A sandboxed Lua API, content mods built from your own install, and a mod format that works with
  Thunderstore. A server can never push code to your PC.
{{% /columns %}}
