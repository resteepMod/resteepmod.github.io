# Get started

ReSteep stands in for Ubisoft's online services for Steep. It runs a small server on your PC, starts the game
against it, and adds an in-game menu and mods. Your own copy of Steep keeps its progression, store, challenges and
rewards, on a server of your own or a friend's.

## What you need

- **Your own copy of Steep** with its final update (TU7), from Steam or Ubisoft Connect. ReSteep checks the game's
  build and only starts a version it knows.
- **Ubisoft Connect** installed. The Steam version of Steep uses it too.
- **Windows 10 or 11**, or a **Linux** desktop (Ubuntu 22.04 or newer, Fedora, Arch, SteamOS, ...).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> ReSteep is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Ubisoft. Using a replacement server or
> mods may breach the terms of service of Ubisoft, Steam or others. You use it at your own risk:
> [Rules and legal stance](../about/rules.md).

## Next steps

1. Install ReSteep and press Play for the first time: [on Windows](windows.md) or [on Linux](linux.md).
2. Unlock the add-ons you bought: [Your DLCs](dlc.md).
3. Optionally, add the store catalogue and challenge rewards with a [game-data pack](../hosting/game-data.md).
4. Then [play with friends](../play/multiplayer.md) and [add mods](../mods/).
