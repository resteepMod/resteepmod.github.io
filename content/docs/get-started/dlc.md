# Your DLCs

ReSteep unlocks the whole base game for everyone. Paid add-ons unlock only for players who bought them: Road to the
Olympics (Japan and Korea), Winterfest, the Extreme Pack, Rocket Wings, the 90's and X Games DLCs and the paid
cosmetic packs.

## Check your add-ons

Run the check once:

- **Windows:** the **Repair** page, **Your DLCs → Check my add-ons**.
- **Linux:** **Settings → Setup & repair**, **Your DLCs → Check my add-ons**.

Ubisoft Connect opens on its own, without ReSteep. Sign in to your **real Ubisoft account** there and close it.
ReSteep then reads which Steep add-ons that account owns from Connect's files. It never sees your password and keeps
only the list of add-ons.

On Windows, the check needs Ubisoft Connect closed first, and ReSteep's older hosts-file entries removed if you
still have them (**Undo everything** in Setup & repair).

## Without the check

Everything that is not paid stays unlocked. Japan also opens with the Japan ticket, which the store sells for
credits. A player in Japan without Road to the Olympics starts in the Alps.

> [!NOTE]
> Paid content stays paid, also through mods and servers: see
