# Playing with ReSteep

Once ReSteep is installed, you play by pressing **Play** in its window. These pages explain what you see on the way:

- [The launcher](launcher.md): the ReSteep window, what Play does, Stop, settings.
- [The in-game menu](in-game-menu.md): ReSteep's menu inside Steep (**N**).
- [Playing with friends](multiplayer.md): joining a friend's server, your account there, switching servers.
- [Progress and saves](progress.md): backups, moving to another PC, starting over.
- [What works](features.md): which of Steep's online features ReSteep provides.
